# Business Questions

## Revenue
1. What is total completed-order revenue?
2. What is monthly revenue and average order value?
3. Which categories and products generate the most revenue?

## Customer
4. Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?
5. Which customers have never ordered?
6. Which customers are one-time vs repeat buyers?

## Growth
7. What is month-over-month revenue growth?
8. Which products and customer segments drive growth or decline?

## Funnel
9. How many customers reach Visit → Signup → AddToCart → Purchase?
10. Where is the largest drop-off?
11. Which acquisition channel converts best?

## Retention & Cohorts
12. What percentage of customers purchase again?
13. Which cohorts retain best?
14. What is the time between purchases?

## Rankings & Contribution
15. Rank customers, products and employees by revenue.
16. What percentage of revenue comes from the top customers?
17. What is cumulative revenue contribution?

## Data Quality
18. Are there duplicates?
19. Are critical fields missing?
20. Are there orphaned customer/product/employee references?
